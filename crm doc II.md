Now let’s break it down properly. 

 

👑 CEO — Vision, Growth & External Control 

 

(Rilwan Bakare Olajuwon) 

 

The CEO’s job is: 

 

“Where is this company going?” 

 

NOT daily rider management. 

 

The CEO focuses on: 

•	Partnerships 

•	Expansion 

•	Investors 

•	Business growth 

•	Revenue strategy 

•	Public image 

 

Departments Under CEO 

 

1️⃣ Growth & Marketing Department 

 

Handles: 

 

•	User acquisition 

•	Promotions 

•	Expansion campaigns 

•	Rider acquisition campaigns 

•	Market research 

 

Why under CEO? 

 

Because growth direction affects: 

•	Revenue 

•	Market positioning 

•	Long-term strategy 

 

2️⃣ Partnerships & Union Relations 

 

Handles: 

 

•	Union negotiations 

•	Institutional partnerships 

•	Government/community relationships 

 

Why under CEO? 

 

These are high-level external relationships. 

 

3️⃣ Finance & Compliance 

 

Handles: 

 

•	Accounting oversight 

•	Financial reporting 

•	CAC/legal compliance 

•	Tax coordination 

•	Budget approvals 

 

⚠️ Early stage: 

This may just be: 

•	External accountant 

•	CEO supervision 

 

4️⃣ Brand & Communications 

 

Handles: 

 

•	Public messaging 

•	Reputation 

•	Media 

•	Brand identity 

 

⚙️ COO — Operations & Execution 

 

(Shitu Damilola Ayomide) 

 

Your role is: 

 

“How do we make this work daily?” 

 

You’re basically: 

•	System execution 

•	Rider ecosystem 

•	Operational coordination 

 

Departments Under COO 

 

1️⃣ Operations Department 

 

(Most important department) 

 

Handles: 

 

•	Rider activity 

•	Coverage zones 

•	Peak-hour management 

•	Daily logistics 

•	Ride fulfillment 

 

Why under COO? 

 

Because operations = execution. 

 

2️⃣ Rider Management Department 

 

Handles: 

 

•	Rider onboarding 

•	Rider verification 

•	Rider discipline 

•	Rider retention 

•	Performance monitoring 

 

Example: 

 

•	High cancellation rates 

•	Low ratings 

•	Settlement issues 

 

3️⃣ Customer Support Department 

 

Handles: 

 

•	Ticket intake 

•	Complaint handling 

•	Escalations 

•	User communication 

 

Why under COO? 

 

Support reflects operational quality. 

 

4️⃣ Trust & Safety Department 

 

Handles: 

 

•	Rider misconduct 

•	Fraud 

•	Safety investigations 

•	Emergency handling 

 

⚠️ Early-stage: 

This may initially be:
